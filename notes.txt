      - CapsuleHitbox
      - BoneHitbox

    - RenderModel:
      - Model (skinned mesh)
      - Animator
      - PlayerRenderMarker
    - CameraHolder:
      - Transform (local offset from player)
        - Camera:
          - Camera
          - Transform (local rotation)
        - PlayerCameraMarker
    - UIHolder:
      - UI elements
      - UIMarker
    - ViewModelHolder:
      - Model (first-person weapon)
      - PlayerViewModelMarker
    - ViewDirectionEntity:
      - ViewDirection (client authoritative, sent to server)
      - ReplicateToServer
      - PlayerViewDirectionMarker
